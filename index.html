What is CX1?

CX1 is a flagship automated trading Expert Advisor (EA) developed for MetaTrader 5 (MT5). It continuously tracks institutional order flow dynamics, delta shifts, and options market structure to execute systematic decisions directly on your MT5 platform.

Is CX1 really free for public use?

Yes, CX1 is completely free for individual retail traders. There are no upfront fees, recurring subscriptions, or hidden charges.

How do I install and use the CX1 bot?

Simply download the .ex5 file directly from our official website or from our Spark Community Discord. Once downloaded, open your MetaTrader 5 terminal, go to File > Open Data Folder > MQL5 > Experts, and paste the file into the Experts folder. Restart or refresh your MT5 Navigator window, and drag CX1 onto your chart. You can watch the step-by-step video tutorial here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-I1dwVs8BCA

What are the system requirements to run the CX1?

OS: Windows 10/11 (64-bit) or Windows Server 2016+.

CPU: Intel i3 / Ryzen 3 minimum (Intel i5 / Ryzen 5 recommended).
RAM: 2 GB minimum (8 GB+ recommended for heavy multi-chart setups).
Storage: 500 MB free disk space.
Network: Stable, low-latency internet connection (VPS recommended for 24/7 uptime).

Can I use CX1 for commercial purposes or signal groups?

Strictly no. CX1 is provided exclusively for personal trading use. Commercial reselling, redistribution, or utilizing CX1 for paid signal channels, copy-trading services, or account management is strictly prohibited.

Can I use CX1 on a prop firm or funded account?

No. We strongly advise using CX1 strictly on personal trading accounts. Most prop firms enforce strict automated risk and copy-trading detection algorithms. If multiple traders execute identical entry prices, stop losses, and take profits, the prop firm's system will flag it as group trading or copy trading, which can lead to account disqualification or a permanent ban.

Is CX1 available for download right now?

Not yet. CX1 is currently in its final stress-testing phase. The official public download link for the MT5 EA will be released soon

Join our official Spark Community Discord. All public download announcements, MT5 setup guides, performance updates, and system logs will be shared directly within our community.
