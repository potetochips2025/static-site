FAQ

You're probably wondering...

What is CX1?

CX1 is our flagship execution Expert Advisor (EA) developed for MetaTrader 5 (MT5). It receives processed real-time feeds from the SPARKS server infrastructure including institutional order flow, delta dynamics, and CBOE options positioning to execute systematic trades with sub-second precision directly on your MT5 platform

Is CX1 really free for public use?

Yes, CX1 is completely free for individual retail traders. There are no upfront fees, recurring subscriptions, or hidden charges.

How do I install and use the CX1 bot?

Simply download the .ex5 file directly from our official website or from our Spark Community Discord. Once downloaded, open your MetaTrader 5 terminal, go to File > Open Data Folder > MQL5 > Experts, and paste the file into the Experts folder. Restart or refresh your MT5 Navigator window, and drag CX1 onto your chart. You can watch the step-by-step video tutorial here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-I1dwVs8BCA

What are the system requirements to run the CX1?

Operating System: Windows 10/11, Windows Server 20

CPU: Intel i3 / Ryzen 3 minimum (Intel i5 / Ryzen 5 recommended).
RAM: 2 GB minimum (8 GB+ recommended for heavy multi-chart setups).
Storage: 500 MB free disk space.
Network: Stable, low-latency internet connection (VPS recommended for 24/7 uptime)

Can I use CX1 for commercial purposes or signal groups?

Strictly no. CX1 is provided exclusively for personal trading use. Commercial reselling, redistribution, or utilizing CX1 for paid signal channels, copy-trading services, or account management is strictly prohibited.

Can I use CX1 on a prop firm or funded account?

No. We strongly advise using CX1 strictly on personal trading accounts. Most prop firms enforce strict automated risk and copy-trading detection algorithms. If multiple traders execute identical entry prices, stop losses, and take profits, the prop firm's system will flag it as group trading or copy trading, which can lead to account disqualification or a permanent ban.

Is CX1 available for download right now?

Not yet. CX1 is currently in its final stress-testing phase. The official public download link for the MT5 EA will be released soon

How can I get notified when CX1 officially releases?

Join our official Spark Community Discord. All public download announcements, MT5 setup guides, performance updates, and system logs will be shared directly within our community.

Our philosophy

Real Data over Broker Noise

We believe that a true trading edge is built on authentic market data, not distorted price feeds or retail indicators. Unlike platforms that rely on off-exchange CFD brokers with arbitrary pricing, our architecture connects directly to centralized futures markets. We reject lagging candlesticks and ungrounded technical indicators. Instead, our philosophy is rooted entirely in raw order flow, options flow, and real-time Gamma Exposure (GEX) data allowing us to decode the true intent and positioning of smart money

Methodology

Edge & Technology Focused

01

Direct Data Ingestion (CBOE & Futures Market)

We pull raw options positioning data directly from the CBOE alongside real-time futures market feeds. This data is streamed straight into our proprietary, in-house server infrastructure for real-time computational analysis

02

Order Flow & Liquidity Filtering

Using specialized order flow engines like Bookmap, we systematically filter out spoofing, phantom liquidity, and fakeouts to measure genuine bid/ask delta shifts and true aggressive volume

03

Structural Context Alignment

Raw market dynamics are evaluated within macro market structure to ensure every potential setup aligns with high-probability structural context before triggering execution parameters

04

Multi-Stage Processing & Global CX1 Routing

When all algorithmic conditions match, our core logic engine converts the data into precise entry models. The trade signal is processed on our dedicated servers and dispatched instantly to our flagship CX1 EA on MetaTrader 5, delivering execution signals to users worldwide

05

Sub-Second Low-Latency Execution

This entire multi-layer pipeline rom raw data extraction to global MT5 chart execution occurs within milliseconds, powered by our custom engineered physical hardware and optimized network architecture
