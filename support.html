Common questions

I cannot see my trades in the client portal. What should I check?

Make sure MT5 is open, the CX1 Expert Advisor is attached to a chart with AutoTrading switched on, and the Sparks Vegeance server address is allowed under Tools > Options > Expert Advisors > Allow WebRequest. Then check that the MT5 account number in the portal matches the account you are logged in to.

Where do I put the CX1 file?

In MT5 choose File > Open Data Folder, open the MQL5 folder, then the Experts folder, and paste the CX1 file there. Restart MT5 or refresh the Navigator and drag CX1 onto a chart.

Do I need to compile CX1?

No. CX1 is delivered ready to run. You only copy it into the Experts folder.

How do I upgrade from CX1 Free to Lite or Pro?

Open the Marketplace in your client portal, choose the plan and pay. The plan activates automatically after the payment is confirmed. You do not need to reinstall anything.

How do I ask for a refund?

Open a ticket from the Support page in the client portal, or email webmaster@sparks.stream with your MT5 account number and payment details. Please read the Refund Policy first.

How long are my trade history and chat history kept in the portal?

Seven days. Before they are cleared you can download both from the portal, so you keep your own copy.

Still stuck? Email webmaster@sparks.stream and include your MT5 account number.
