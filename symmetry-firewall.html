What Symmetry Firewall does

Everything you need to keep attackers out, with nothing to learn first.

Blocks attacks on sight

Script injection, SQL injection and file-path tricks.

Expand

Every request is read before your website sees it. Attack payloads are stopped at the door and logged, so your pages, forms and database never process them.

  • Script injection (XSS)
  • SQL injection
  • Path and file probing

Catches scanners and probes

Bots hunting for weak spots get shut out.

Expand

Automated scanners, known bad browsers and requests for pages that should not exist are recognised quickly. You can add your own paths and agent names to the list.

Stops password guessing

Repeated failed sign-ins lead to a ban.

Expand

Sign-in pages are watched for repeated attempts, and your own website can report wrong passwords to the firewall too. Guessers are banned before they get lucky.

Bans that grow

60 minutes, then 4 hours, then 16 hours, up to 30 days.

Expand

First offence gets a short ban. Every return multiplies it by four, up to your chosen maximum. Real visitors who make a mistake are back quickly; attackers are not.

A console you can read

Threats over time, top attackers, most targeted pages.

Expand

See what was aimed at your site, from which address and which page, with plain-language charts. Unban a visitor, add your own address to the allow list, or pause protection in one click.

Safe by design

Never locks you out, never breaks your site.

Expand

If the firewall cannot reach its licence check, your site simply carries on without it. Your own address can be allow-listed, and removing the firewall takes two clicks. It also reads the real visitor address behind Cloudflare and other proxies.

No firewall can promise to stop every attack. Symmetry Firewall is one layer of protection; keep your website software and passwords up to date as well.

Up and running in four steps

You need a website that runs PHP 8.1 or newer, and your licence key.

  1. Upload the folder

    Copy the symmetry-firewall folder into your website's main folder.

  2. Activate

    Open the install page, enter your licence key and choose a console sign-in.

  3. Switch on protection

    One button turns protection on for your website, and every site folder beside it.

  4. Watch it work

    Open the console and press Test protection. Threats stopped appear within minutes.
