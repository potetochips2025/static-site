Privacy Policy

What personal data SPARKS collects, why, and your rights.

Terms of ServicePrivacy PolicyRefund PolicyCookie Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data SPARKS ("we", "us") collects when you use the client portal and the CX1 copier, why, who sees it and what rights you have. We handle personal data as required by the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its rules, and we apply the same care to clients in other countries.

1. What we collect

DataWhy
Portal login: username, email, password (stored only as a one-way hash), registration time, last sign-in timeTo create and secure your account
Trading account details sent by your copier: MT5 account number, account name, broker, server, currency, leverage, balance, equity, margin, open and closed copied positions, trade history and execution reportsTo copy trades, show your dashboard, apply plan limits and your protection settings, and investigate problems
Technical data: IP address, browser type, copier version, time of requests, security events (for example failed sign-ins)To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse and fix faults
Support: your chat messages and tickets, and who answered themTo help you
Plan and marketplace records: your plan and its end date, downloads, "notify me" requests, the date you accepted the TermsTo provide your plan, show what you may download and prove you agreed
Payment records: the amount, date, payment reference and status of a payment for a planTo activate your plan, keep accounting records and handle refunds

We do not collect your MT5 trading password, investor password or broker login. We never ask for them. Your card, e-wallet or bank details are entered with the payment provider (for example PayMongo) and are not stored by us; we only receive the payment reference, amount and status. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

2. Cookies

We use only the cookies the site needs to work and stay secure:

  • Session cookie keeps you signed in. It is deleted when you close the browser.
  • Security cookie is a random code that changes while you use the site. It lets us detect if a stolen copy of your session cookie is used elsewhere, and then we sign you out.
  • Cookie choice remembers what you chose in the cookie notice (kept for 6 months).
  • Colour look (optional) remembers the colour look you picked. It is saved in your browser only if you allow preferences.

Details and how to change your choice are in the Cookie Policy.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies and we do not use analytics. The client portal loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers to fetch them. Why we may use your data (lawful basis)

To perform our agreement with you (providing the Service); to follow the law; for our legitimate interests in security, preventing fraud and improving the Service; and, where needed, with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

4. Who receives it

  • Our authorised staff and administrators, who see only what they need to support you. Some account details may be hidden from staff.
  • Service providers that run the Service for us, for example Cloudflare (network and tunnel), our hosting, and the payment provider that processes your payment (for example PayMongo), which has its own privacy policy. They process data only to provide those services.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Our servers or providers may be in a country other than yours. Where data leaves your country we use providers that protect it appropriately.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active and for as long as needed afterwards to meet legal, accounting and security needs and to handle disputes. Trade history and support chats shown in the client portal are kept for 7 days by default and then deleted automatically; you can download a copy from the portal before then. Security logs are kept for a limited period and then deleted or anonymised. Encrypted backups are kept on a rolling schedule (daily, weekly and monthly copies) and age out automatically.

6. How we protect it

Passwords are hashed. Connections use HTTPS. Sessions are protected by secure, HTTP-only cookies with an extra rotating security code, and sign-ins are rate limited. Backups are encrypted. Access by staff is limited and logged. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your data safe and will tell you and the authorities about a breach as the law requires.

7. Your rights

You may ask to be informed about how your data is used; to access it; to correct it; to have it erased or blocked where the law allows; to object to or limit certain uses; to receive a copy in a common format (portability); and to withdraw consent. You may also complain to the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines (privacy.gov.ph) or to your local data authority. To use any right, email webmaster@sparks.stream or use the Support page in your client portal. We may need to confirm it is really you first.

8. Children

The Service is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has registered, tell us and we will remove the data.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way we will update the date above and, where needed, ask you to accept the new version at your next sign-in.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: email webmaster@sparks.stream or use the Support page in your client portal.
