Refund Policy

When a refund can be approved and how it is paid.

Terms of ServicePrivacy PolicyRefund PolicyCookie Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

This Refund Policy applies to purchases from SPARKS ("we", "us"). It is part of the Terms of Service. We sell software licences (plans); we do not manage anyone's trading money, so trading results are never a ground for a refund.

1. What is free and what is paid

CX1 Free and the CX1 Open-source framework cost nothing, so there is nothing to refund. Lite (15 days) and Pro (30 days) are prepaid software plans of fixed length. You may download the Lite or Pro file before paying, but the plan is only activated on your account after payment is confirmed. The premium API (when released) is a paid product and is covered by this policy and by the terms published with it.

2. When a refund can be approved

You may ask for a refund of a Lite or Pro plan within 7 days after we activate it, if the conditions below are met. A refund is never the full amount you paid (see section 3).

  • The request is made through the Support page, with your MT5 account number and the payment details.
  • The Service could not be used for a reason on our side (for example we could not activate the plan, or a fault on our servers stopped the copier for a significant part of the plan), and we could not fix it in a reasonable time; or you bought the wrong plan and have not used it to copy trades.
  • For a change of plan within the window we may instead move your payment to the other plan.

Refunds are decided by our team case by case. Paying does not by itself create a right to a refund.

3. Refunds are not full: payment fees are deducted

Every payment passes through a payment provider (for example PayMongo) that charges a fee when the money comes in to us, and may charge another fee when money is sent back. These fees are not refundable. An approved refund is therefore the amount we actually received for the plan, minus the payment-provider fee charged on your payment, minus any fee charged to send the refund back to you (including bank, e-wallet or transfer charges). The refund will be less than what you paid, and we will tell you the exact amount before we send it.

4. What is not refundable

  • Payment-provider and transfer fees, in and out (section 3).
  • Trading profits or losses, slippage, spreads, broker fees, swaps or any loss made while the copier was running. We do not hold or manage your trading money, so we cannot refund or compensate trading results.
  • Time already used after the refund window, or a plan that was used to copy trades, except where we caused the problem.
  • Problems caused by your computer, VPS, internet, broker, MT5 settings or by not following the install steps.
  • Accounts suspended or ended for breaking the Terms.
  • Chargebacks: if you dispute a payment with your bank or provider instead of contacting us, we may end the plan, and any fees the dispute costs us may be kept.

5. How a refund is paid

Approved refunds are paid back by the same method you paid with, where possible, usually within 7 to 14 days of approval. When a refund is paid, the plan on your account ends and the account returns to Free.

6. Your legal rights

This policy does not take away any right you have under the consumer laws that apply to you.

7. Contact

To ask for a refund or ask a question: email webmaster@sparks.stream or use the Support page in your client portal.
