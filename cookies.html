Cookie Policy

The cookies and browser storage used by Sparks Vegeance.

Terms of ServicePrivacy PolicyRefund PolicyCookie Policy
This website (sparks.stream) sets no cookies, loads no analytics or advertising and fetches nothing from other sites: fonts and images are served from this site. The sections below describe the client portal.

Last updated October 22, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains the small files and browser storage that the Sparks Vegeance client portal uses, why, and how you can control them. It is part of our Privacy Policy.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Browser storage (such as "localStorage") works in a similar way. We use them only to run the portal safely and to remember your choices.

2. What we use

NameKindWhat it doesHow long
Session cookieStrictly necessaryKeeps you signed in as you move between pages.Until you close the browser or sign out
Security cookieStrictly necessaryA random code that changes while you use the site. It lets us detect a stolen copy of your session and then sign you out.Until you close the browser or sign out
svm_ccStrictly necessaryRemembers the choice you made in the cookie notice, so we do not ask again.6 months
svm-skinPreferences (optional)Remembers the colour look you picked (Blue, White, Orange, Lime and others). Kept long term only if you allow preferences; otherwise it is kept only for the current browser session so the look stays the same after you sign in.Until you clear your browser data, or until you close the browser if preferences are off

3. What we do not use

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, social media cookies or analytics. We do not sell data collected through cookies.

The client portal loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers to fetch them.

4. Your choices

  • Accept All Cookies allows the strictly necessary cookies and the optional preferences.
  • Manage Preferences lets you allow the strictly necessary cookies only. The site works fully; it simply does not remember your colour look next time.
  • Strictly necessary cookies cannot be switched off, because the portal cannot keep you signed in or stay secure without them.
  • You can change your choice at any time: press Cookie settings at the bottom of the sign-in page, or delete the site's cookies in your browser settings. Browsers also let you block cookies; if you block the strictly necessary ones, you will not be able to sign in.

5. Changes

If we change the cookies we use, we will update this page and the date above, and ask for your choice again where needed.

6. Contact

Questions about cookies: email webmaster@sparks.stream or use the Support page in your client portal.
