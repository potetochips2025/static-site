CX1: the Sparks Vegeance trade copier for MetaTrader 5

One Expert Advisor, one client portal and a marketplace. CX1 mirrors trades from the Sparks Vegeance server on your own MT5 account, with limits you control.

What you get

Everything needed to copy, monitor and manage trades in one place.

CX1 Expert Advisor

The copier that runs inside your MT5 terminal.

Expand

CX1 receives execution parameters from the Sparks Vegeance server and opens, modifies and closes the matching positions on your account. It only needs an internet connection and your MT5 account number.

  • Runs on your PC or VPS
  • Key issued automatically, you never type it
  • Order settings follow MetaTrader 5's own functions

Client portal

Live equity, a risk meter and every copied trade.

Expand

Sign in to see your account equity, open positions, your copied trade history and your plan status. Download your records, chat with support and manage your profile and password.

  • Light and dark colour looks
  • Trade history and chat export
  • Built-in support chat

Marketplace

Download CX1 and upgrade your plan.

Expand

The Marketplace lists the CX1 files and what each plan needs. Paid plans activate automatically after payment is confirmed, with no manual approval.

Loss limit

Pause copying when you say stop.

Expand

Set a maximum loss and CX1 stops copying new trades once it is reached, so one bad day cannot run unattended.

Secure by design

Encrypted connections and account protection.

Expand

Sign-ins are rate limited, suspicious addresses are suspended, and your trading data is only visible to you and to support staff you contact.

Support

Real people, in the portal.

Expand

Ask a question from the Support page in the portal. If you have not linked an MT5 account yet you can still reach our team.

Install CX1 in five steps

You do not need to compile anything or write any code.

  1. Create an account

    Sign up in the client portal and open the Marketplace.

  2. Download CX1

    Choose CX1 Free (or your paid plan) and download the file.

  3. Copy it into MT5

    In MT5: File > Open Data Folder > MQL5 > Experts. Paste the CX1 file there.

  4. Allow WebRequest

    Tools > Options > Expert Advisors: allow WebRequest for the Sparks Vegeance server address shown in the portal.

  5. Attach to a chart

    Drag CX1 onto any chart with AutoTrading on. Your account appears in the portal.
