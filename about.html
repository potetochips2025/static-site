An independent team of traders and engineers

Sparks Vegeance is an independent trading technology and research project founded by an active collective of market participants, system programmers and execution specialists.

What is Sparks Vegeance

We build and operate proprietary execution infrastructure that bridges institutional market structure, such as order flow and options dynamics, and systematic trading automation.

The people who write the software are the people who trade it. That keeps the product honest: if a feature is not useful at the desk, it does not ship.

Access

Development
